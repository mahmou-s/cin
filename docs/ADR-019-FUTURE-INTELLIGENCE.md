# ADR-019 — Future Intelligence + Scenario-Aware Questioning

## Decision
CIN will explicitly model desired future, planning horizon, scenario preferences and readiness barriers, then use them to prioritize questions that reduce uncertainty and reveal actionable capability/opportunity gaps.

## Non-goals
- No automatic prediction of future outcomes.
- No claim that a selected scenario is probable.
- No conversion of a readiness signal into fact without evidence.

## Ranking
Future alignment 30%, scenario relevance 20%, readiness gap 20%, future opportunity 15%, capability context 5%, uncertainty reduction 10%.
