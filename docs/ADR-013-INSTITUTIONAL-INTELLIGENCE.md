# ADR-013 — Institutional Intelligence Benchmarking

## Status
Accepted — design research track.

## Decision
CIN will maintain a primary-source institutional intelligence study of BlackRock and selected German asset managers. The study is a design-learning benchmark, not a performance ranking.

## Method
Each source is transformed through:
`Source → Observed Practice → General Principle → CIN Adaptation → Candidate Module → Evidence`.

## Initial institutions
- BlackRock
- DWS
- Union Investment
- Deka Investment

Later candidates may include Allianz Global Investors, Flossbach von Storch, Metzler, Helaba Invest, HANSAINVEST and Ampega.

## Guardrail
Publicly documented practice must be distinguished from CIN's own inference. No unsupported claim about proprietary internal systems is to be treated as fact.
